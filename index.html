Needs attention

Outliers in the current returns. Each is a specific question for a named station.
24MURDERS
Murders are up to 24 from 19 in the same months last year, while overall crime is down a fifth. Kuppam sub-division has 9 of the 24 — Kuppam Urban 4, Rallabudugur 3 — though it registers only about an eighth of district crime.
280ROAD DEATHS
Road deaths have already passed last year: 280 to 27 September against 269 for January–September 2025. Accidents overall are down 10% (469 against 522), so the problem is severity, not volume — fatal accidents 254 against 247.
6POCSO
Six POCSO cases are past the two-month investigation limit without a charge-sheet. Nagari 3, Palamaner 2, Kuppam 1 (as on 23·09). Of 45 POCSO cases under investigation, 31 are already charged and waiting to be taken on file.
233KUPPAM U
Kuppam Urban carries 233 UI cases — the district's heaviest, and unchanged in a month. V.Kota is down to 178 from 189 and Vedurukuppam to 174. These three still hold a quarter of all station pendency.
90V.KOTA
V.Kota has 90 of the district's 662 charged cases still waiting on a CC number — the largest share, though down from 104. S.R.Puram is next at 60. PS registers show 585 against CCTNS 662, a 77-case gap to reconcile.
35EXECUTED
35 warrants executed in the first 18 days of September — slower than August's pace of 52. Rallabudugur leads with 5. 185 criminal and 105 NI Act warrants remain (return as on 18·09).

Sorted by crime-against-women score. Gap column shows where the two themes diverge for the same station.
StationCrime vs womenGanja GapLatest month
Read this tab with more caution than the others. Sample sizes swing hard month to month — from 1,181 respondents district-wide in Jul-25 to 8,803 in Feb-26, then back to about 2,300 by mid-2026. A station's monthly figure can rest on as few as 20–30 responses, so single-month movements are mostly noise. Judge on the trend line, not the last point.

Known defect in the source: in the crime-against-women series, Dec-25 and Jan-26 are identical for all 31 stations — same percentages and the same 7,710 respondent count. That is a duplicated month, not a flat one. The ganja series does not have this problem. Worth having the survey team confirm before either month is quoted.
