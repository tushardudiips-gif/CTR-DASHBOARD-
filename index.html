+192UI CASES
Cases under investigation rose by 192 since 27 September, to 2,584 — all of the rise is in this year's cases. 2026 cases still with the IO went from 1,196 (27 Sep) to 1,427 while older years fell by 39. Load per investigating officer is heaviest at Vedurukuppam (44.3 station-side cases per IO), Kuppam Urban (21.3), K.Nagar (18.5) and Vijayapuram (17.8), against 7.2 district-wide.
324ROAD DEATHS
Road deaths reached 324 in January–October, against 292 last year. Accidents are down 10% (521 against 582) but fatal accidents are up, 295 against 269. Yadamari (10 → 21), Puthalapattu (26 → 38), V.Kota (24 → 34) and Thavanampalle (14 → 22) rose most.
5/5PUTHALAPATTU
Puthalapattu fails all five checks in the crime review. Property detection 3 of 11, recovery 13%, 36% of its UI from 2024 or earlier, 8.4 station-side cases per IO, and accidents up 46% with only 24 e-challans per accident.
101V.KOTA
V.Kota has 101 of the district's 673 charged cases still waiting on a CC number. S.R.Puram 59, Vedurukuppam 46. PS registers show 534 (16 Nov) against 673 on CCTNS — Kuppam Urban's register shows 4 against 36, Vijayapuram's 5 against 23.
25MURDERS
Murders are up to 25 from 20 (January–September) while attempts to murder halved, 26 from 50. Kuppam sub-division has 9 of the 25 murders. Worth checking that serious assaults are not being registered under lesser hurt sections.
0CHEATING
Seven stations registered no cheating or breach-of-trust case in nine months. N.R.Pet, Gudipala, Thavanampalle, Rompicherla, Gudupalli, Vijayapuram and Palasamudram, while the district registered 95. Check NCRP complaints against FIRs.

Read this tab with more caution than the others. Sample sizes swing hard month to month — from 1,181 respondents district-wide in Jul-25 to 8,803 in Feb-26, then back to about 2,300 by mid-2026. A station's monthly figure can rest on as few as 20–30 responses, so single-month movements are mostly noise. Judge on the trend line, not the last point.

Known defect in the source: in the crime-against-women series, Dec-25 and Jan-26 are identical for all 31 stations — same percentages and the same 7,710 respondent count. That is a duplicated month, not a flat one. The ganja series does not have this problem. Worth having the survey team confirm before either month is quoted.
